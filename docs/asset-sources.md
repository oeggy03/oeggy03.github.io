# Company mark sources

The three added company marks are local raster assets. Each remains in its supplied colors and is contained in a white tile in the experience timeline.

| Company | Local asset | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TikTok | `src/assets/company-logos/tiktok.png` | [TikTok developer logo pack](https://sf16-va.tiktokcdn.com/obj/eden-va2/uvzhqeh7nuhd/tt4d/logo-pack.zip), linked from the [official design guide](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/getting-started-design-guidelines) |
| GIC | `src/assets/company-logos/gic.png` | [GIC official apple-touch icon](https://www.gic.com.sg/content/themes/gic_2021/assets/favicons/apple-touch-icon.png) |
| Uptale | `src/assets/company-logos/uptale.png` | [Uptale official 282px brand icon](https://www.uptale.io/assets/favicon-282x282-d7317ac046.png) |

The TikTok square icon was extracted from its official logo pack. The GIC mark is the official square site icon, and the Uptale mark is the official square brand icon. These files are downloaded from company-controlled web properties; they are not recreated or recolored.
