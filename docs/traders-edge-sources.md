# Trader’s Edge case study sources

The Trader’s Edge copy and figures on the portfolio case study are drawn from the two project reports supplied for this work. The final-year report and the shorter CA report contain overlapping figures. Five distinct figures are used in total; the image assets are direct extracts of the report figures, with surrounding page text and page furniture excluded.

The figures were visually reviewed at their extracted dimensions before selecting them. The fifth figure explains the distinction made in the copy: level-authored event effects configure simulated price movement, while multiplayer headlines are reported as a social tool for influencing other players’ decisions.
